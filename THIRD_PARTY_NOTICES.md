## mydatalakehouse OSF synthetic-population normalizer

`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/osf_tables.py` and
`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/osf_ducklake.py` are derived from
`mydatalakehouse/osf_synthetic_ducklake.py` at commit
`4a9687de19ad192b97139f085d3e348dfe187cbd`.

`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/profile_population.py` is derived
from the Colorado quality, pilot, and fixture modules in `mydatalakehouse` at
commit `ec2336af55fb7221922ad972ec1b5f750746f114`.

`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/atus.py` is derived from the ATUS
staging and normalization modules at commit
`653250f6789f66315bf442a7252ab8bac85f3277` and the donor-matching module at
commit `844cb7eba28ff4697b7606899a2eef2eb7bf4193`.

`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/profile_schedules.py` is derived
from `mydatalakehouse/colorado_front_range_schedules.py` at commit
`00380e58c1a33449d07bd346ce6c0df3eb6ceaf1`.

`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/destination_supply.py` is derived
from the Colorado OSM POI module at commit
`ecb3b0c729f546c2db1dd4d2eeeab98add6d9c93` and the destination-assignment
portion of the routing module at commit
`f9b0d2eb9fa830f7004eec1c95c3bdc6bef4abb6`.

`casmsocial/datasets/colorado_front_range/profile_runtime.py` is derived from
the Colorado routing and feasibility modules at commit
`f9b0d2eb9fa830f7004eec1c95c3bdc6bef4abb6` and the CASMSocial export module
at commit `ec2336af55fb7221922ad972ec1b5f750746f114`.
